I am a technology leader with a strong background in enterprise software, product platforms, and cloud architecture.
Currently, I work as **Manager Technology at ZNAPZ**, part of the **[Naviam Group](https://www.naviam.io)**, where I am
responsible for the technology direction, architecture, and alignment of products and engineering practices across the
organization.

My work focuses on enterprise asset management solutions, with deep expertise in **IBM Maximo and IBM MAS**, cloud
platforms, and scalable system design. I operate at the intersection of product strategy and technology execution:
defining roadmaps, overseeing product development, aligning tooling and governance, and ensuring platforms are robust,
secure, and fit for enterprise-scale use.

Alongside strategic responsibilities, I remain closely involved in complex technical challenges, supporting teams and
customers with performance analysis, system stability, and critical issue resolution. I have extensive experience
working with distributed teams, integrating organizations and platforms, and operating in regulated, enterprise
environments.

I am particularly interested in platform modernization, product-driven engineering, and the practical application of
automation and intelligence in enterprise software, always with a strong focus on reliability, maintainability, and
business value.

Born and raised in Izhevsk, Russia, I discovered my interest in programming early on. I earned a Master’s degree in
Computer Science from **St. Petersburg National Research University of Information Technologies, Mechanics and Optics 
(ITMO)** and began my professional career in St. Petersburg in 2006 before relocating to the Netherlands. Over the years,
I have worked with diverse clients, led distributed teams, and delivered complex, long-running enterprise platforms.
